General information only. Figures are estimates based on the inputs entered. All scenarios use the same loan to value ratio, interest rate and running costs, with interest only lending. The model excludes depreciation, the Medicare levy, vacancy, purchase costs and principal repayments. Without negative gearing, rental losses are assumed to be quarantined and carried forward against future rental income or capital gains, subject to the final legislation. Bottom and recovery values are affordability indicators, not forecasts, and markets can overshoot in either direction. This is not tax, financial or credit advice. Clients should obtain advice from their accountant and a licensed adviser before acting.
